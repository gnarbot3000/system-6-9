Public summary

System 6.9

Community DIY endless shuttle powered by battery or hard-wired. Open software. Big-box builds. Size the motor and pack for your use case.

Mission

System 6.9 is a community project: open-source software plus builds from materials you can get online or at Home Depot, Harbor Freight, Lowe’s, Menards, and similar. The center of gravity is community and community development — not a finished commercial tow product.

Each builder has their own use case. Motor size and battery pack (or hard-wired supply) are chosen by that use case — we do not ship a one-size powertrain.

One-liner

DIY endless shuttle for the community — one architecture, many surfaces. Water (foil/skim), backyard snow, sled uphill, skate tow, and more. You size the motor and pack for your use. We publish the software and sell the awkward bits (control board, Dyneema, traveler hardware). Use at your own risk.

Same endless loop. Different accessories, mounts, and speed presets.

Weak fits (don’t lead with these): high big-air wake (wants tall towers), full-size cable parks, public ski-area lifts (certification world).

What we sell

  • Control box — hardware brain only (MCU, power-stage interface, pack input path, E-stop, remote bay, weather enclosure). No motor or pack inside.
  • Accessories — Dyneema loop kits, capstan/drum, carrier + handle, return idler, tension pack, A-frame/mount kits, fairleads and anchors.
  • Lifestyle — apparel, stickers, caps, lake-day merch. Funds the community.

What we don’t sell

  • Motors (e.g. BLDC 80100-class)
  • ESCs (e.g. VESC-class)
  • Batteries and chargers (e.g. 12–14S high-C packs)
  • Remotes (community-supported options)

You source and size these for your use case. Compatible BOM lives on GitHub; community can PR alternatives.

Software

  • Shuttle firmware — open source, free to download and flash from GitHub
  • Docs + CAD — open (license finalize at repo launch)
  • Community — issues and PRs improve soft start, turn points, end-of-run, dual-site presets
  • Warranty on code — none. Use at your own risk. Not a certified tow system.
